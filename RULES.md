# Flow Lines — Rules Document

_Authoritative source of truth. If the implementation conflicts with this
document, fix the implementation._

## 1. Objective

Connect every matching pair of dots on the board with a pipe, without any
pipes crossing or overlapping, and fill **every** square of the board.

## 2. Setup

- A square board (5×5 Easy, 7×7 Classic/Medium/Daily, 9×9 Hard).
- N pairs of colored dots ("flows") are placed on squares. Both dots of a
  pair share a color.
- Classic pack: 15 handcrafted 7×7 boards, 4–7 flows.
- Endless tiers / daily: boards are generated by carving the whole grid into
  random self-avoiding walks (a valid solution always exists by construction).

## 3. Turn order

Flow Lines is a single-player puzzle — there are no turns. The player draws
pipes in any order.

## 4. Legal moves

- Press on a dot (or on any square of an already-drawn pipe) and drag
  orthogonally (up/down/left/right) through empty squares.
- Extending a pipe into an empty square.
- Dragging back over your own pipe shortens it (backtrack).
- Tapping a dot of a connected pipe clears that pipe so it can be redrawn.
- Undo restores the board to the state before the last grab.
- Reset clears all pipes (dots stay).

## 5. Illegal moves

- Drawing diagonally (drags snap to orthogonal steps).
- Extending into a square occupied by another flow's pipe or dot.
- Extending into a square occupied by your own dot that is not the matching
  partner (loops are not allowed).
- Any input while the level-complete celebration is playing.

## 6. Captures

Not applicable — no captures in Flow Lines.

## 7. Special rules

- A pipe is **connected** when its drawn path runs from one dot of the pair
  to the other.
- A level is **complete** only when ALL pipes are connected AND every board
  square is filled. Connected-but-gappy is not a win ("Fill every gap!").
- Hints: the engine reveals the next steps of the least-progressed flow with
  a progressive animated ghost trail. Free players get 5 hints/day; PRO is
  unlimited.
- Stars: 3 stars if the level is cleared in ≤ (number of flows) strokes,
  2 stars if ≤ 2× flows, else 1 star. A stroke = one drag gesture that
  changed the board.

## 8. Scoring

- Levels flooded (completed), total pipes connected, daily streak, stars per
  level (best kept implicitly by play).

## 9. Winning conditions

All pairs connected + board 100% filled → celebration → victory card with
stars, Next / Replay / Menu.

## 10. Draw conditions

Not applicable.

## 11. AI strategy

Not applicable (no opponents). Generated boards use a randomized tiling
generator; the tiling itself is the solution, so every generated board is
guaranteed solvable.

## 12. Edge cases

- Grabbing a square of a completed pipe clears it (redraw allowed).
- Grabbing mid-pipe trims the pipe back to the grabbed square.
- Releasing a drag mid-board keeps the partial pipe.
- Undo during a hint reveal cancels the reveal first.
- Backgrounding the app pauses engine timers; the watchdog re-arms the
  current phase on resume — a stuck celebration or hint can never freeze
  the game.
- Classic boards carry no stored solution; hints there use a BFS nudge
  toward the matching dot through empty squares.

## 13. Test cases

- All 15 classic levels: every dot index in range, no duplicate squares.
- Generator: full board coverage, disjoint flows, pairs valid, deterministic
  per seed (same seed → same board).
- Engine: blocked moves rejected, backtrack trims, undo restores, win only
  on full+connected, hint reveal is progressive (not instant).
- Settings: player names persist as one JSON string; legacy StringList key
  migrates once and is removed.
